# GreenCart AI
**A Multimodal Sustainability Assistant for the Indian Market**

GreenCart AI is a specialized tool designed to help consumers reduce their environmental footprint. By leveraging Gemini 1.5's vision-language capabilities, the application analyzes shopping lists and images to provide sustainable alternatives tailored to the Indian context.

---

## Project Overview

GreenCart AI bridges the gap between consumer intent and environmental action. It identifies high-impact products and suggests "Green-Swaps" that are price-matched in INR and explained through relatable cultural analogies.

### Core Objectives
- **Accessibility:** Eliminate complex data entry through direct image/text analysis.
- **Contextualization:** Provide pricing in Indian Rupees (₹) and recognize local brands.
- **Education:** Translate abstract carbon data into understandable metaphors and anecdotes.

---

## Features

- **Multimodal Processing:** Users can upload images of physical items or input typed shopping lists.
- **Eco-Scorecard:** Every item is assigned a score (1-10) based on carbon footprint, water usage, and packaging.
- **The Green-Swap Engine:** Logic-driven recommendations for eco-friendly alternatives within a ±20% price parity.
- **Relatable Insights:** Swaps include metaphors (e.g., comparing energy savings to a ceiling fan's usage) to make impact tangible.
- **Market Integration:** Provides direct search queries for real-time pricing and purchasing on Indian e-commerce platforms.

---

## Technical Stack

- **AI Model:** Gemini 1.5 Pro (Multimodal Vision & Reasoning)
- **API Integration:** Google AI Studio (Generative AI SDK)
- **Search Logic:** Grounded web search for live price tracking
- **Environment:** Python 3.9+ / Node.js 18+
